# Long-term research and evidence plan

Status: ongoing strategic plan; feature execution paused at the engineering gate.
Updated: 2026-10-03. This plan preserves the longer horizon alongside the
[near-term engineering plan](PLAN.md). [STATE](STATE.md) identifies current focus
and mode; the [plan registry](README.md#plans-and-work-status) links all areas.
This is planning knowledge, not a new implementation handoff or permission to
resume paused features. Work one assigned task at a time.

## Completed work and evidence

- The synthetic reliability/planning/Copilot core and local Lab are integrated.
  [Core readiness evidence](READINESS_REVIEW.md) records bounded acceptance.
- Research packages for paid warmup, retention, supply/policy comparisons,
  observed sales/calibration, lost sales, physical counts and hosted readiness
  are integrated. [Combined acceptance](RESEARCH_INTEGRATION.md) retains outcomes,
  negative findings and original evidence; these do not establish production benefit.
- Engineering readiness investigation and a benchmark proposal are complete.
  [Assessment](ENGINEERING_READINESS.md); implementation remains gated in PLAN.

## Remaining gaps and sequence

1. **Immediate prerequisite:** review and execute the assigned engineering program
   only after workload, environment and budget approval. Report its capacity and
   remaining runtime/recovery gaps before selecting feature work.
2. **Later execution evidence:** preserve paused planner-loop work; resume only
   with an explicit handoff after the engineering decision. Actual source/planner
   gates, repeated clocks and independent execution outcomes remain unaccepted.
3. **Decision-quality follow-ups:** choose a concrete unresolved service/owned-stock
   weakness and freeze fresh evidence before revisions. Existing holdouts are
   consumed; advanced models, allocation and supplier constraints remain conditional.
4. **Public access and portfolio:** the [hosted plan](HOSTED_LAB_PLAN.md) retains
   completed readiness design and release gaps. Real runtime/recovery evidence and
   reviewed host/domain/cost precede deployment. Refresh claims from exact evidence.
